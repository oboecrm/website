# Modeling accounts and deals

If you only model two things first, make them `Account` and `Deal`.

That combination is enough to validate:

- object naming conventions
- required and unique fields
- relation handling
- API and admin expectations

## Recommended first cut

- `Account.name`
- `Account.domain`
- `Deal.title`
- `Deal.value`
- `Deal.stage`
- `Deal.account`

## Why this works

It gives you one durable company record and one workflow-heavy commercial record without dragging you into secondary concepts too early.

## What to avoid initially

- dozens of nullable fields
- deeply nested workflow states
- custom objects before core objects are legible
