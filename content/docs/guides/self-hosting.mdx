# Self-hosting

Self-hosting is not just a deployment checkbox. It changes the trust model of the product.

## Why it matters

- your team owns infrastructure and runtime decisions
- compliance and residency choices stay in-house
- CRM changes can follow the same operational standards as the rest of your stack

## What to validate early

- database ownership
- secret management
- deployment strategy
- backup and migration expectations

## When self-hosting is the right fit

Choose this model when control, auditability, and architectural consistency matter more than outsourcing operational responsibility.
